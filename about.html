We didn't set out to build a brand. We set out to fix a problem. The aftercare on the market wasn't good enough for the work we were putting in.

How It Started

THE PROBLEM
WITH AFTERCARE

In 2019, two Manchester tattoo artists sat in their studio after a long day and had the same frustration. Their clients were using generic moisturisers, petroleum-based ointments, or nothing at all — and their work was suffering for it.

Colour faded too fast. Healing took longer than it should. The products on shelves weren't made for tattooed skin.

So they started from scratch. Working with a cosmetic chemist and a panel of UK dermatologists, they spent 18 months formulating a range that actually worked — one designed specifically for the biology of healing tattooed skin.

KONG launched in 2019 with a single product: the Tattoo Butter. Within 6 months it had spread across UK studios entirely by word of mouth. No paid ads. No influencer deals. Just artists telling artists.
