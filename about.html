Our Story

Built For Artists

Kong Tattoo Care was founded by tattoo artists who were tired of recommending products that weren't good enough. So we made our own.

The Beginning

Why We Built Kong

In 2019, our founder — a professional tattoo artist with over a decade of experience — noticed a gap in the market. The aftercare products his clients were using weren't formulated for the specific needs of tattooed skin. Generic moisturisers, unscented creams repurposed from other industries, and products that looked great on the shelf but underperformed in practice.

He partnered with a UK-based cosmetic chemist and spent two years in formulation and testing before the first Kong product launched. Every formula was tested on real tattooed skin, refined based on feedback from artists and collectors, and checked by dermatologists before going to market.

Kong is now trusted by studios across the UK, Europe, and Southeast Asia. Every product is still made in the UK to the same standard that launched the brand.

What We Stand For
Our Values
01
Formulated for Tattoos
Every Kong product is designed specifically for tattooed skin — not adapted from generic skincare. Our formulas address the actual requirements of the healing and maintenance process.
02
Artist First
We work directly with professional tattoo artists in product development, testing, and feedback. If it doesn't meet the standards of working professionals, it doesn't make the range.
03
Dermatologist Tested
All Kong products are independently tested by dermatologists before launch. Skin safety is non-negotiable. We test every formula on sensitive skin and track adverse reaction rates.
04
Made in the UK
Manufacturing in the UK gives us direct oversight of quality at every stage. We know exactly what goes into every product and where every ingredient comes from.
05
Honest Formulas
We publish full ingredient lists and explain what each ingredient does. No marketing fluff, no proprietary blend mysticism — just transparent formulation.
06
Long-Term Thinking
The products that work best aren't always the ones that sell best in the short term. We build for efficacy and long-term skin health, not for trend cycles.
