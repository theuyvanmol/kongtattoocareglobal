How It Works

THE KONG METHOD

01

Numb

Apply our Numb Cream 45 minutes before your session. Reduces discomfort for longer sittings, so your artist can do their best work.

02

Cleanse & Renew

Within 3 hours of your session, gently cleanse with our Foam Cleanser. Follow with Tattoo Butter while skin is still damp.

03

Glow

Once healed (2–4 weeks), introduce our Glow Serum. Vitamin C complex brightens and maintains colour saturation long-term.

04

Defend

Daily SPF protection with our Defend range. UV is ink's #1 enemy — protect your investment and keep your art looking fresh.
